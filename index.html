EIGHT FRONTIERS

One continuous thesis, moving across the technologies that define the deep future.

01Quantum

Quantum Computing

Israel has emerged as a global leader in quantum technology, building strength across both hardware and software. Each domain carries immense potential for groundbreaking innovation - hardware is redefining the limits of what quantum systems can achieve, while software is unlocking their true computational power.

02Robotics

Robotics and Drones

Autonomous systems are transforming logistics, inspection, defense, and labor. We invest in robotics companies where software and hardware meet precision execution.

03Data Centers

Data Centers

The physical layer of the internet is under reinvention - energy, cooling, latency, control. We invest in the core technologies and operational systems powering that shift.

04Space

Space

Low-Earth orbit is no longer experimental - it’s operational. We back companies building communication infrastructure with clear commercial and national-security use cases.

05Accelerators

Particle Accelerators

A once-niche technology now powering material innovation, medicine, and clean energy. We invest where complex physics meets commercial possibility.

06Cyber

Cyber & Attack Surfaces

As the digital threat surface expands, we invest in platforms securing infrastructure, devices, and networks across civilian and defense domains.

07Sensing & RF

Sensing, RF, Optics & Quantum Intelligence

From RF to quantum, sensors are foundational to autonomy, defense, and scientific discovery. We invest in sensing platforms enabling high-resolution, real-time intelligence across domains.

08Energy

Geothermal & Nuclear Energy

Decarbonization needs scalable, always-on energy. We invest in geothermal and nuclear innovations redefining what’s possible in clean, resilient power infrastructure.
